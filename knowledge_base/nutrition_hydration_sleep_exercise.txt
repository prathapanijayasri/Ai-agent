# WHO and MedlinePlus Guide: Nutrition, Hydration, Sleep, and Physical Activity
Source: World Health Organization (WHO) Guidelines & MedlinePlus Healthy Living
Topic: Wellness Foundations and Preventive Health

## 1. Daily Hydration Guidelines
Water is vital for temperature regulation, joint lubrication, nutrient transport, and waste excretion.
Recommended Daily Fluid Intake:
- General guideline: Approximately 2.0 to 2.5 liters (8 to 10 cups) of fluid per day for women, and 2.5 to 3.5 liters (10 to 12 cups) per day for men, from all beverage and food sources.
- Requirements increase during hot climates, high humidity, intense physical exertion, pregnancy, lactation, or illness with fever, vomiting, or diarrhea.
Hydration Indicators:
- Pale straw or light yellow urine indicates healthy hydration.
- Dark amber urine, dry mouth, headache, and decreased urination frequency suggest dehydration.
Best Fluid Sources:
- Plain water is the ideal fluid choice without added sugars or calories.
- Herbal teas, milk, water-rich fruits (watermelon, oranges, cucumber), and clear broths also contribute.

## 2. Evidence-Based Nutrition Principles
A balanced dietary pattern reduces the risk of noncommunicable diseases including diabetes, heart disease, and stroke.
Key Components:
- Vegetables and Fruits: Eat at least 400 grams (5 portions) of diverse fruits and vegetables daily, excluding starchy roots like potatoes.
- Dietary Fiber: Consume at least 25 to 30 grams of fiber per day from legumes, lentils, whole grains (brown rice, oats, whole wheat), nuts, and seeds.
- Fats: Keep total fat intake below 30% of total energy. Replace saturated fats (butter, lard, fatty meats) and trans fats with unsaturated fats (olive oil, avocados, nuts, fatty fish).
- Free Sugars: Limit free sugars to less than 10% (ideally under 5%) of total caloric intake.
- Sodium: Maintain sodium intake below 2,000 mg per day (equivalent to about 1 level teaspoon of salt).

## 3. Sleep Hygiene and Restorative Sleep
Adequate sleep is fundamental for cognitive performance, hormonal regulation, and immune competence.
Sleep Recommendations:
- Adults (aged 18-64): Require 7 to 9 hours of quality sleep per night.
- Older adults (65+): Require 7 to 8 hours.
Evidence-Based Sleep Hygiene Rules:
- Consistency: Maintain identical wake-up and sleep times every day, including weekends.
- Light Exposure: Maximize morning natural sunlight exposure and minimize blue-light screens (phones, computers, TVs) 1 to 2 hours before bed.
- Sleep Environment: Keep the bedroom dark, quiet, and cool (approximately 18°C or 65°F).
- Stimulants: Avoid caffeine 6 hours before bedtime and limit evening alcohol, which disrupts rapid eye movement (REM) sleep architecture.

## 4. Physical Activity Recommendations
Physical activity is a key determinant of energy expenditure, cardiovascular health, and musculoskeletal strength.
Weekly Minimums for Adults:
- Aerobic Exercise: At least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity (e.g., brisk walking, swimming, light cycling), OR 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity (e.g., running, fast cycling).
- Strength Training: Muscle-strengthening activities involving all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week.
- Sedentary Behavior: Reduce prolonged sitting; stand up and move for 2 to 5 minutes every hour of desk work.
